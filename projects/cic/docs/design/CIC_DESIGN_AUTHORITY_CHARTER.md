# CIC DESIGN AUTHORITY CHARTER
# Document ID: CIC-DESIGN-AUTHORITY-CHARTER-v1.0
# Owner: CIC Design Authority
# Status: Active
# Scope: All CIC surfaces, assets, and communications

---

## 0. Mission
The CIC Design Authority exists to ensure that **every CIC surface expresses a unified, operator‑grade identity** across:

- CIC Dashboard  
- CIC Docsify Console  
- CIC Memos  
- CIC Login  
- CIC Websites  
- CIC Documents  
- CIC Slide Decks  
- CIC Social Media  
- CIC Internal Correspondence  

The Authority governs **visual language**, **structural standards**, **semantic consistency**, and **design compliance** across the entire Cast Iron Charlie ecosystem.

---

## 1. Mandate
The CIC Design Authority has full jurisdiction over:

### 1.1 Design System Ownership
- [CIC Design System v1.0](CIC_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md)  
- CIC Component Library  
- CIC Icon Set  
- CIC Layout System  
- CIC Documentation Templates  
- CIC Slide Templates  
- CIC Social Media Templates  

### 1.2 Semantic & Structural Intelligence
- Glossary Auto‑Linker  
- API Change Detector  
- Release Intelligence Layer  

### 1.3 Enforcement
- [CIC Design Policy v1.0](CIC_DESIGN_POLICY.md)  
- [CIC Design Review Workflow](CIC_DESIGN_REVIEW_WORKFLOW.md)  
- CIC Design Compliance Audits  

---

## 2. Authority Structure

### 2.1 Design Authority Lead
Responsible for:
- Final approval of all CIC design decisions  
- Maintaining the CIC Design System  
- Overseeing enforcement and audits  
- Managing escalations  

### 2.2 Design Review Council
A rotating group of operators responsible for:
- Reviewing submissions  
- Ensuring compliance  
- Recommending updates to the Design System  

---

## 3. Responsibilities

### 3.1 Define
- Establish and maintain CIC’s visual and structural language  
- Publish versioned updates to the Design System  
- Define templates for documents, slides, and social media  

### 3.2 Enforce
- Ensure all CIC assets comply with CIC Design Policy v1.0  
- Reject non‑compliant submissions  
- Require corrections and resubmission  

### 3.3 Govern
- Approve new components, icons, and templates  
- Manage design exceptions  
- Conduct quarterly design audits  

---

## 4. Jurisdiction

### 4.1 Mandatory Review Surfaces
All assets must undergo Design Authority review before publication:
- UI surfaces  
- Documentation  
- Slide decks  
- Social media posts  
- Internal memos  
- Release notes  
- API documentation  
- Architecture diagrams  

---

## 5. Escalation Path
1. **Level 1** — Design Review Council (Minor violations)
2. **Level 2** — Design Authority Lead (Major violations)
3. **Level 3** — Executive Escalation (Architectural impact)
