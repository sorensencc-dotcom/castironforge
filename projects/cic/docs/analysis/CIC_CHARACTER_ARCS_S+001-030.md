# CIC Character Arcs (S+001–030)
**Project:** Cast Iron Charlie
**Scope:** 1881–1960
**Purpose:** Tracing the narrative and emotional transformation of the primary cast.

---

## 1. Charles Sorensen (The Production Architect)
*   **Arc**: The Immigrant Genius to the Uncredited Titan in Exile.
*   **Rise (1905–1927)**: From machinist to the architect of the assembly line and the Rouge. The "Cast Iron Charlie" persona emerges—tough, brilliant, indispensable.
*   **The Squeeze (1927–1940)**: Stuck between Henry’s paranoia and Bennett’s shadow state. The Soviet deal and the Rouge expansion prove his global scale.
*   **The Masterwork (1941–1943)**: The Willow Run hotel sketch. He builds the impossible (1 bomber/hour) while his authority is being pried loose.
*   **Exile & Legacy (1944–1968)**: Resignation, Willys-Overland, the Jeep, and the Cuba expropriation. He writes his memoir, dies uncredited by the Ford machine, but remains the man who built the 20th century.

## 2. Henry Ford (The Paternalistic Titan)
*   **Arc**: The Visionary Disruptor to the Paranoiac Shadow.
*   **Vision (1903–1918)**: Disrupts the world with the Model T. Sorensen is his primary weapon.
*   **Decline (1920s–1940s)**: Retreats into nostalgia and paranoia. Undermines Edsel, empowers Bennett, and resists the very modernization Sorensen and Edsel champion.
*   **Legacy**: Dies leaving a company in chaos, saved only by his grandson and the systems Sorensen built.

## 3. Edsel Ford (The Modernizer Undermined)
*   **Arc**: The Refined Successor to the Tragic Martyr.
*   **Vision**: Attempts to modernize Ford’s aesthetics and labor relations (Lincoln, Mercury).
*   **Conflict**: Systematically crushed by his father. His health declines alongside his authority.
*   **Impact**: His death in 1943 is the inflection point that forces HFII’s return and Sorensen’s eventual exit.

## 4. Harry Bennett (The Shadow Enforcer)
*   **Arc**: The Sailor to the Shadow Governance.
*   **Rise**: Leverages Henry’s paranoia to build the Ford Service Department—a private police force.
*   **Conflict**: The primary antagonist for Sorensen and Edsel. Represents the coercion-based control regime.
*   **Fall**: Fired by HFII in 1945, marking the end of the pre-war security state.

## 5. Henry Ford II (The Reformer)
*   **Arc**: The Navy Lieutenant to the Corporate Savior.
*   **Rise**: Returns in 1943 to a dying company. Purges the old guard (Bennett) and clears the path for the Whiz Kids.
*   **Impact**: Orchestrates the 1956 IPO and transitions Ford from a family shop to a global corporation.

## 6. Robert McNamara (The Systems Man)
*   **Arc**: The Statistical Control Officer to the Rational Architect.
*   **Impact**: Introduces quantitative management. The Falcon (success) vs. the Edsel (failure). 
*   **Legacy**: Represents the final shift from Sorensen’s craft-based intuition to the metrics-driven regime.
