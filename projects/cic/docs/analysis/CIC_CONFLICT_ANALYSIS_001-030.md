# CIC Conflict Analysis (001–030)
**Scope:** GAPs 001-030 (1881-1960)
**Type:** One-Page Mental Model / Operator Summary

---

## 1. The Succession War (Authority)
At the top of the graph is a succession war: Henry → Edsel → HFII → McNamara. The edges show that authority at Ford is never cleanly handed off—it’s pried loose. 
*   Henry crushes Edsel’s autonomy, then turns on Sorensen under Bennett’s influence.
*   HFII later has to break that same Bennett/old‑guard network to modernize the firm. 

**Pattern:** Every modernization wave starts as a purge.

## 2. Industrial and Labor Control (The Middle Band)
Sorensen vs. Bennett and Production Engineering vs. the Sociological Department encode the fight over who really governs the factory: engineers, security, or moral police. 
*   **UAW vs. Ford Division:** Externalizes the control struggle into the street—first as open violence, later as a negotiated constraint on management power. 

**Pattern:** The graph makes it obvious that Ford’s “efficiency” story is inseparable from coercion.

## 3. Strategy and Systems (The Outer Ring)
*   **GM vs. Ford Division:** GM’s pressure sets the stage for the Edsel: a misread of segmentation amplified by internal turf war (Ford Division vs. Edsel Division). 
*   **Falcon vs. Edsel Division:** This is the pivot. McNamara weaponizes systems analysis to produce the anti‑Edsel (the Falcon). In doing so, he proves that numbers can overrule both family instinct and division politics. 
*   **Whiz Kids vs. Legacy Executives:** The same move at the financial layer: spreadsheets displacing gut.

## 4. Synthesis: The Master Narrative
Put together, the graph says this:
Ford’s 80‑year arc is a sequence of **power realignments where new control technologies—mass production, security, accounting, systems analysis—arrive as conflicts first and “efficiency” only later.** 

Sorensen, Bennett, HFII, the Whiz Kids, and McNamara aren’t just characters; they are carriers of successive control regimes. The documentary spine you’ve built is the story of those regime changes, told through who gets to point the machine and who gets ground up underneath it.
