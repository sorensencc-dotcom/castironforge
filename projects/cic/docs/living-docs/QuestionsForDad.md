## Section 1 — Facts to Confirm

**Q1** — Charles Emil's death date: Find a Grave says August 11, 1968; Wikipedia says August 28. Which is correct?
**Dad's answer:** Most references point to August 11th, unable to exactly confirm.

**Q2** — RESOLVED — What do you know about Clifford M. Sorensen — Charles Emil's only son? Did you know Charles Emil remarried in 1960 (Edith Thompson Montgomery)?
**Dad's answer:** Clifford M. Sorensen owned Continental Die Casting Corporation. Sole supplier to Ford Motor Co for die cast trim parts. During WWII switched to military armament. After war nearly died in corporate aircraft accident. Recovered, sold company, divorced, moved to Cuba. Died in Cuba but was buried in Woodland Park North Cemetery in Miami.

**Q3** — RESOLVED — Helen (Helene E. Mitchell, born 5 March 1880, died 4 March 1959 in Miami). What do you know about her background, how they met, what she was like?
**Dad's answer:** She worked in the Accounting Department at the Detroit Stove Works on E. Jefferson Ave in Detroit. Always caring and reserved, loved her grandchildren. Interred in Woodland Park North Cemetery mausoleum in Miami next to him.

**Q4** — Do you know the addresses of Charles Emil's homes — Detroit Towers, St. Clair River cottage, Miami Beach winter house?
**Dad's answer:** Sorensen never lived in Grosse Pointe. Michigan residences: The Detroit Towers, 6th floor on Jefferson Ave, 1929–1944. Smaller cottage on the St Clair River in Algonac. Winter home at 5185 N. Bay Dr in Miami Beach, Florida. The Florida residence recently sold for just under $10,000,000.
