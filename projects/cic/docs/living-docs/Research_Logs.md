### Editorial Notes

The Willys chapter presents a structural parallel: Sorensen was pushed out at Ford in 1944 after winning a war, joined Willys, built the strategy that saved the Jeep brand, and was sidelined again by 1946 — a different board making the same mistake as Ford family management. The pattern is not anomalous. It is the pattern.

The Utility Line vehicles Sorensen designed — the pickup, the station wagon, the panel truck — survived and multiplied into the 1960s and beyond. The modern SUV is, in a direct line of descent, a Sorensen design. His name is on none of it.

**Note on "single afternoon in 1945" framing** in website copy and treatment: Farm Collector places the CJ-2A launch at CESOR Farm in July 1945 but does not support a "single afternoon" timeline for the civilian Jeep decision. Verify against This Week magazine primary source (W-005) before using in narration.
